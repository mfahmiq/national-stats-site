# Concept tournament — best-national-statistics-site-20261126-0539

Judge criteria in weight order: (1) working site in screenshots — pages/charts/sections
reached by links, real figures, every control working; (2) content & usefulness — figures
with source and year; (3) ease of use — nav, phone layout, readability; (4) look &
originality; (5) speed & structure — small first load, split source files.

12 concepts, pairwise, first criterion on which they differ decides:

1. Indonesia deep dive — one country, 7 linked pages, BPS/World Bank figures
2. World explorer — 190 countries, compare any two
3. Single-page KPI dashboard — one country, one page
4. Historical trends 1960–2024 — one country, decade selector
5. Regional atlas — tile-grid map of provinces, click a region
6. Economy-first investor site — GDP, trade, budget only
7. People-first — demographics, health, education only
8. Narrative report — "State of the Nation 2025", chapter story
9. Global rankings — sortable tables of 190 countries
10. Quiz + explorer — "Guess the country" game
11. SDG tracker — 17 goal cards with progress bars
12. Multi-country atlas — 12 country pages

Results:
- 1 vs 2: 1 wins on (1) — deeper linked pages with complete figures; 2 spreads thin,
  near-identical thin pages. 1 advances.
- 1 vs 3: 1 wins on (1) — brief demands pages reached by links, not one page.
- 1 vs 4: 1 wins on (1)/(2) — more sections than trends alone.
- 1 vs 5: 1 wins on (2)/(3) — tile map is striking (4) but hard to read shrunk and
  province-only data is narrower. 5 is the strongest challenger on (4).
- 1 vs 6: 1 wins on (2) — broader content answers more visitor questions.
- 1 vs 7: 1 wins on (1)/(2) — 7 is a subset of 1's scope.
- 1 vs 8: 1 wins on (1) — linear narrative, few working controls; 8 wins (4) but (1) outranks.
- 1 vs 9: 1 wins on (1)/(3) — dense 190-row tables unreadable when shrunk; thin per-country data.
- 1 vs 10: 1 wins on (1)/(2) — quiz is a gimmick, not what a statistics visitor needs.
- 1 vs 11: 1 wins on (1)/(2) — SDG scope narrower, progress bars repeat.
- 1 vs 12: 1 wins on (2)/(3) — 12 thin country pages vs one deep country with provinces.
- Challenger check: 5 vs 8 — 5 wins on (1) (working map controls vs narrative);
  5 vs 2 — 5 wins on (2) (complete province figures vs thin country rows).
  Runner-up: concept 5 (regional atlas).

CHAMPION: concept 1, "NusaStat — Statistik Indonesia", with concept 5 folded in as the
Provinces page (searchable, sortable 38-province table + province detail card) and
concept 4 folded in as trend charts on the Economy page.
